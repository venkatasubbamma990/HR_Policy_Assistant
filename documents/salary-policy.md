## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how employee compensation is structured, calculated, disbursed, and revised at NovaTech Solutions. It ensures transparency, compliance with the Payment of Wages Act, 1936, and internal pay equity.

---

| Basic Salary | Fixed monthly base pay | 40% |

## 3. Pay Cycle & Disbursement

- **Pay Period:** 1st to last day of calendar month  
- **Pay Date:** Last working day of each month (or 28th if month-end is a holiday)  
- **Mode:** Direct bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) only  
- **Payslip Access:** HRMS portal → My Payroll → Download PDF  

**Late Joiners:** Salary is pro-rated: `(Monthly Gross / Total Days in Month) × Days Worked`

**Example:** Joined on 15 June (30-day month), Monthly Gross ₹1,20,000  
Pro-rated salary = (1,20,000 / 30) × 16 = **₹64,000**

## 4. Statutory Deductions

| Deduction | Rate / Rule |
|-----------|-------------|
| Employee PF | 12% of Basic (max ₹1,800/month if basic capped at ₹15,000 for PF) |
| Professional Tax | ₹200/month (Karnataka) |
| Income Tax (TDS) | As per IT slabs and declared investments |
| ESI | Applicable if gross ≤ ₹21,000/month (employee 0.75%, employer 3.25%) |

Employees must submit Form 12BB and investment proofs by **January 31** each year.

## 5. Annual Increment & Appraisal

- **Appraisal Cycle:** April 1 – March 31 (performance year)  
- **Increment Effective:** July 1 each year  
- **Typical Increment Range:**

| Performance Rating | Increment Range |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| Exceeds Expectations (5) | 12% – 18% |
| Meets Expectations (4) | 8% – 12% |
| Partially Meets (3) | 3% – 6% |
| Below Expectations (2) | 0% – 2% |
| Unsatisfactory (1) | 0% (PIP may apply) |

**Promotion:** May include 15%–30% CTC revision plus band change, effective on promotion date.

---

## 6. Variable Pay & Bonuses

### 6.1 Performance Bonus (Annual)

- Eligibility: Employees who complete **≥ 6 months** in the performance year  
- Payout: August each year  
- Target bonus: **10% of annual CTC** at "Meets Expectations"  
- Formula: `Target Bonus × Company Performance Factor (0.8–1.2) × Individual Rating Factor`

**Example:** CTC ₹20,00,000, Rating 4, Company factor 1.0  
Bonus = 2,00,000 × 1.0 × 1.0 = **₹2,00,000** (paid in August)

### 6.2 Joining Bonus

- Offered selectively for critical hires  
- Typical range: **₹50,000 – ₹5,00,000**  
- Clawback: 100% if employee exits within 12 months; 50% if within 18 months

## 8. Salary Revision on Role Change

- Internal transfer with band change: effective from transfer date, pro-rated in that month  
- Demotion (rare): revised CTC not below previous basic compliance minimum  
- All revisions require HRBP + Department Head approval  

---

## 9. Full & Final Settlement (FnF)

On exit, FnF includes:

1. Pro-rated salary for days worked  
2. Unused earned leave encashment (max 30 days)  
3. Gratuity (if eligible — 5+ years continuous service)  
4. Pending reimbursements (approved only)  
5. Deduction: notice period shortfall, asset recovery, loan outstanding  

**FnF Timeline:** Within **45 days** of last working day (typically 30 days for voluntary exits).

---

## 10. Confidentiality

Salary information is confidential. Employees must not disclose their own or others' compensation except to HR, their manager (for approval workflows), or legal/tax advisors.
