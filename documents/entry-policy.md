# Employee Entry & Onboarding Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** HR-POL-ENT-004  
**Version:** 3.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies To:** All new hires, rehires, and transfers from acquisitions

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the pre-joining, joining-day, and onboarding process to ensure compliant, consistent, and positive employee entry into NovaTech Solutions.

---

## 2. Offer & Pre-Joining

### 2.1 Offer Letter Components

Every offer includes:

- Designation and department  
- CTC breakdown (see Salary Policy HR-POL-SAL-001)  
- Joining date and work location  
- Probation period (**6 months** standard)  
- Notice period (**60 days** for L1–L4; **90 days** for L5+)  
- Contingencies: background verification (BGV), document verification  

### 2.2 Pre-Joining Timeline

| Days Before Joining | Action |
|---------------------|--------|
| T-14 | Offer acceptance via e-sign portal |
| T-10 | Upload documents to onboarding portal |
| T-7 | BGV vendor initiates checks |
| T-3 | IT creates email; welcome email sent |
| T-1 | Joining instructions (time, floor, POC) |
| T-0 | Joining day |

### 2.3 Required Documents (India)

| Document | Mandatory |
|----------|-----------|
| Aadhaar Card | Yes |
| PAN Card | Yes |
| Passport-size photographs (2) | Yes |
| Previous employment relieving letter | Yes (experienced hires) |
| Last 3 months payslips | Yes (experienced hires) |
| Degree certificates | Yes |
| Address proof (current + permanent) | Yes |
| Bank account proof (cancelled cheque/passbook) | Yes |
| Form 11 (PF declaration) | Yes |
| Form F (nomination) | Yes |

**Fresher exception:** Relieving letter and payslips waived; college ID and degree provisional accepted.

---

## 3. Background Verification (BGV)

| Check | Typical Turnaround |
|-------|-------------------|
| Identity (Aadhaar/PAN) | 1–2 days |
| Employment (last 2 employers) | 5–10 days |
| Education (highest degree) | 5–7 days |
| Criminal (current address) | 7–14 days |
| Global database (sanctions) | 2–3 days |

**Joining condition:** Employee may join while BGV is in progress.  
**Failure outcome:** If BGV fails materially within **30 days** of joining, employment may be terminated without notice per offer terms.

**Example BGV failure:** Fake degree claim, undisclosed criminal conviction, employment dates falsified by > 6 months.

## 5. Probation Period

| Parameter | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Duration | 6 months (may extend by 3 months max) |
| Confirmation review | Between month 5 and 5.5 |
| Notice during probation | **30 days** (employee or employer) |
| Benefits during probation | CL, SL only; no PL until confirmation |
| Confirmation criteria | Performance, conduct, attendance ≥ 95% |

**Early confirmation:** Possible at 4 months for exceptional performers (manager + HRBP approval).

**Extension example:** Employee rated 2.8/5 at month 5 → 3-month extension with documented improvement plan.

## 6. Onboarding Program (First 90 Days)

### 6.1 Milestones

| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| Week 1 | Tools, access, policy training, meet team |
| Week 2–4 | Role-specific training, shadow assignments |
| Month 2 | First independent deliverables |
| Month 3 | 30-60-90 review with manager |
| Day 90 | Onboarding survey + probation mid-check (informal) |

### 6.2 Buddy Program

- Every new hire assigned a **buddy** (tenure ≥ 1 year)  
- Buddy responsibilities: 2 check-ins/week in first month, weekly in month 2–3  
- Buddy incentive: ₹2,000 gift card if new hire completes 90 days  

### 6.3 Mandatory Trainings (First 30 Days)

| Training | Duration | Deadline |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Information Security | 2 hours | Day 7 |
| Code of Conduct & POSH | 1.5 hours | Day 7 |
| Anti-bribery & corruption | 45 min | Day 14 |
| Data privacy (GDPR/DPDP overview) | 1 hour | Day 14 |
| Role-specific compliance | Varies | Day 30 |

Non-completion blocks HRMS self-service after deadline + 7 days grace.

## 8. Rehire Policy

| Gap Since Exit | Treatment |
|----------------|-----------|
| < 6 months | Prior service not counted; new employee ID |
| 6 months – 2 years | HRBP review; prior tenure may count for leave accrual |
| > 2 years | Fresh hire; no tenure credit |

**Not eligible for rehire:** Terminated for misconduct, BGV failure, or on do-not-hire list.

---

## 9. Acquisition / Transfer Entry

When joining via company acquisition:

- **Day 1 legal employer:** NovaTech Solutions  
- **Integration period:** 90 days parallel systems access  
- **Tenure credit:** As defined in integration MOU (typically full tenure for leave/gratuity)  
- **Benefits alignment:** NovaTech benefits effective from integration date or next quarter  

---

## 10. No-Show & Joining Date Change

| Scenario | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| Joining date change | Allowed once, up to **30 days** from original date (offer validity) |
| No-show without intimation | Offer withdrawn; 6-month rehire block |
| No-show with intimation | Case-by-case HR review |

---

## 11. Employee Data

Personal data collected at entry is used for payroll, statutory compliance, and benefits only. Employees may update address, bank, and emergency contact via HRMS → My Profile.

**Emergency contact:** Mandatory on Day 1.
