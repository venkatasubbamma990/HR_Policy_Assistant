## 1. Overview

NovaTech provides paid and unpaid leave categories to support employee wellbeing, statutory compliance, and work-life balance. All leave must be applied through the HRMS portal **at least 3 working days in advance** (except emergencies).

## 3. Privilege Leave (PL)

- **Accrual:** 1.5 days per month (18/year), credited on last day of each month  
- **Minimum application:** Half-day increments allowed  
- **Maximum consecutive PL:** 15 working days (longer requires VP approval)  
- **Carry forward:** Up to 10 PL days to next calendar year; excess lapses on March 31  
- **Advance PL:** Employees with ≥ 1 year tenure may apply up to 5 days PL in advance against future accrual  

**Example Accrual:**  
Employee joins 1 July → 6 months × 1.5 = **9 PL days** credited by 31 December.

---

## 4. Casual Leave (CL)

- For personal errands, short absences  
- **Max consecutive CL:** 3 days  
- Cannot be combined with PL in the same week without manager approval  
- **Lapse:** All unused CL expires on December 31 — no carry forward  

---

## 5. Sick Leave (SL)

- For illness or medical appointments  
- **SL > 2 consecutive days:** Medical certificate required within 48 hours of return  
- **SL > 5 consecutive days:** HR must be notified; fitness certificate required before return  
- Manager may request telephonic check-in for SL exceeding 3 days  

---

## 6. Compensatory Off (Comp Off)

Earned when an employee works on:

- Declared public holiday (company-wide)  
- Weekend day approved for critical release/deployment  

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Approval | Pre-approved by manager + HR for holiday/weekend work |
| Credit | 1 Comp Off per full day worked (half-day = 0.5) |
| Expiry | Must be used within **60 days** of earning |
| Max balance | 5 Comp Off days at any time |

**Example:** Worked Saturday 8 March (full day) → Comp Off credited 10 March → Must use by **9 May**.

---

## 7. Maternity & Paternity Leave

### Maternity (Female employees)

- **Duration:** 26 weeks (182 calendar days) for first two children  
- **Third child onwards:** 12 weeks  
- **Eligibility:** 80 days worked in 12 months preceding expected delivery date  
- **Pay:** Full salary for 26 weeks  
- **Work from home:** Optional phased return — up to 4 weeks WFH after maternity leave ends (manager approval)

### Paternity

- **Duration:** 10 working days  
- **Window:** Within 30 days of child's birth or adoption  
- **Eligibility:** Permanent employees only  

---

## 8. Bereavement Leave

- **5 days** paid leave on death of: spouse, parent, child, sibling, grandparent, parent-in-law  
- **3 days** for extended family (uncle, aunt, cousin) — manager discretion  
- No carry forward; apply within 7 days of event  

---

## 9. Marriage Leave

- **5 days** paid leave  
- **Once per employment tenure**  
- Apply at least **15 days in advance**  
- Proof (marriage invitation/certificate) may be requested  

---

## 10. Leave Without Pay (LWP)

- When all paid leave is exhausted  
- Requires **Department Head + HRBP** approval  
- Impacts: salary (pro-rated deduction), increment eligibility, bonus pro-ration  
- Max continuous LWP: **30 days**; beyond that requires CHRO approval  

**LWP Salary Deduction Formula:**  
`Daily Rate = Monthly Gross / 30`  
Deduction = Daily Rate × LWP Days

## 12. Leave Application & Approval Workflow

1. Employee submits request on HRMS  
2. Manager approves within **2 working days**  
3. Auto-escalation to skip-level if no action in 48 hours  
4. Emergency leave: Inform manager within **4 hours** of shift start, formalize on HRMS within 2 days  

**Negative balance:** Not permitted except pre-approved advance PL.

---

## 13. Leave During Notice Period

- **Privilege Leave:** Cannot avail PL during notice period  
- **Sick Leave:** Allowed with medical certificate; may extend notice if SL > 3 days  
- **Encashment on exit:** Unused PL encashed at basic salary rate, max **30 days**  

**Encashment Example:**  
Basic = ₹50,000/month, 22 unused PL days  
Daily basic = 50,000 / 30 = ₹1,666.67  
Encashment = 1,666.67 × 22 = **₹36,666.74**
