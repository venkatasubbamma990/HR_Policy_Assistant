# Work From Home (WFH) & Hybrid Work Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** HR-POL-WFH-003  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies To:** All employees unless role is designated "Office-First"

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines eligibility, limits, and expectations for remote and hybrid work arrangements at NovaTech Solutions post the return-to-office transition.

---

## 2. Work Models

| Model | Description | Default For |
|-------|-------------|-------------|
| **Hybrid** | 3 days office / 2 days WFH per week | Engineering, Product, Design, Marketing |
| **Office-First** | 5 days office; WFH exception only | Facilities, Reception, Lab staff |
| **Remote-First** | 4–5 days WFH; quarterly office visits | Pre-approved roles (max 10% of headcount) |
| **Full Remote** | Permanent remote, no fixed office seat | Rare; VP + HR approval |

---

## 3. Hybrid Schedule (Standard)

### 3.1 Default Pattern

| Day | Location |
|-----|----------|
| Monday | Office |
| Tuesday | Office |
| Wednesday | WFH |
| Thursday | Office |
| Friday | WFH |

- **Core office hours:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM IST (must be reachable)  
- **Team sync day:** Tuesday and Thursday — teams may mandate in-office attendance  
- **Flex swap:** Employees may swap WFH days within the same week with manager approval (HRMS → WFH Calendar)

### 3.2 Monthly Limits

| Rule | Limit |
|------|-------|
| Max WFH days/month (Hybrid) | 10 days |
| Max consecutive WFH days | 3 days (except approved Remote-First) |
| Min office days/month (Hybrid) | 12 days |
| Extra WFH beyond quota | Requires HRBP approval |

---

## 4. Eligibility

### 4.1 Automatic Eligibility (Hybrid)

- Confirmed employees (post probation)  
- Performance rating ≥ 3 (Partially Meets) in last cycle  
- Role not marked "Office-First" in HRMS  

### 4.2 Not Eligible

- Employees in first **90 days** of employment (office full-time except pre-approved relocation)  
- Active Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)  
- Roles requiring secure on-prem infrastructure (specified in offer letter)  

### 4.3 Remote-First Criteria

All must be true:

1. Role can be performed 100% remotely  
2. Employee tenure ≥ **12 months**  
3. Manager + HRBP + VP sign-off  
4. Employee has dedicated workspace and stable internet (≥ 50 Mbps)  

---

## 5. WFH Application Process

1. Log in to HRMS → **Work Location** → Select dates  
2. Manager auto-notified; approval within **24 hours**  
3. For recurring pattern: Set "Default Hybrid" once per quarter  
4. **Same-day WFH:** Allowed max **2 times/month** for emergencies (health, home repair) — notify manager by 9 AM  

---

| Item | Company Provides | Employee Responsibility |
|------|------------------|-------------------------|
| Laptop | Yes | Care & return on exit |

| Keyboard/Mouse | Reimburse up to ₹3,000/year | Submit invoice |
| Internet | ₹1,500/month reimbursement | Submit bill monthly |
| Chair/Desk | No | Employee arranges |
| Power backup | No | Recommended for critical roles |

**One-time WFH setup allowance:** ₹10,000 for Remote-First employees (desk, chair, accessories — receipt required within 30 days of approval).

---

## 7. Productivity & Availability Expectations

While on WFH:

- Online on Slack/Teams during core hours (10 AM – 4 PM IST)  
- Respond to messages within **30 minutes** during core hours  
- Camera on for scheduled video meetings  
- Available for ad-hoc calls with **15 minutes notice**  
- Must not perform WFH from outside India without **15 days prior** HR + Legal approval (tax/compliance)  

**Attendance tracking:** HRMS auto-marks WFH on approved days; biometric not required.

---

## 8. Data Security & Compliance

- Use company VPN for all work systems (mandatory)  
- No public Wi-Fi without VPN  
- Screen lock when away (max 5 min idle)  
- Confidential calls in private space — no cafés for sensitive discussions  
- Violation may result in WFH privilege revocation  

---

## 9. WFH During Special Circumstances

| Scenario | Policy |
|----------|--------|
| Mild illness (contagious) | WFH allowed up to 3 days without SL if manager approves |
| City-wide transport strike | Company may declare optional WFH day (announced by 8 AM) |
| Personal emergency | 1–2 days ad-hoc WFH; formalize on HRMS within 48 hours |
| Parental/caregiving | Up to 4 WFH days/month with HRBP approval (12-month max) |
| Pregnancy (medical) | Doctor's note → up to 30 days flexible WFH before maternity leave |

---

## 10. Office-First Role Exceptions

Office-First employees may WFH only for:

- Approved medical quarantine (with certificate)  
- Company-declared emergency (pandemic, office closure)  
- Pre-approved personal event (max **4 days/year**)  

---

## 11. International WFH / Workation

- **Not permitted by default** — tax and labor law risk  
- Short business travel: Follow travel policy  
- "Workation" (working from another city/country): Requires **30 days prior** written HR + Legal approval; max **14 calendar days per year**  
- Destinations on restricted list (updated quarterly) are not allowed  

---

## 12. Revocation of WFH Privileges

WFH may be suspended or revoked if:

- Performance drops below expectations  
- Repeated unapproved absences from office  
- Security incident attributable to remote work negligence  
- Failure to meet availability standards  

**Process:** Written notice from HRBP → 2-week office full-time trial → reinstatement review.

---

## 13. Examples

**Example 1 — Hybrid employee in March (22 working days):**  
- Default WFH: 8 days (2 per week × 4 weeks)  
- Used 2 same-day emergency WFH  
- Total WFH: 10 days ✅ (within limit)  
- Office days: 12 ✅  

**Example 2 — Request denied:**  
Employee on PIP requests 3-day WFH block → **Denied** (PIP restriction).
