# Notice Period Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** HR-POL-NOT-006  
**Version:** 2.3  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies To:** All employees; supplements Exit Policy HR-POL-EXT-005

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy standardizes notice period requirements during employment, resignation, termination, and role changes. It ensures adequate transition time for knowledge transfer and business continuity.

---

## 2. Standard Notice Periods

### 2.1 By Employee Band

| Band | Level | Notice (Employee Resigns) | Notice (Company Terminates)* |
|------|-------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| L1 | Intern / Trainee | 15 days | 15 days |
| L2 | Associate | 30 days | 30 days |
| L3 | Engineer / Analyst | 60 days | 60 days |
| L4 | Senior / Team Lead | 60 days | 60 days |
| L5 | Manager | 90 days | 90 days |
| L6 | Director | 90 days | 90 days |

*Excludes summary dismissal for gross misconduct (no notice).

### 2.2 Special Categories

| Category | Notice Period |
|----------|---------------|
| Probation (unconfirmed) | 30 days (either party) |
| Probation (confirmed early at 4 months) | Band-standard applies from confirmation date |
| Contract staff | As per contract (typically 15–30 days) |
| Remote-First (approved) | Same as band; WFH during notice allowed with manager approval |

## 3. When Notice Period Starts and Ends

### 3.1 Start Date

Notice period begins on the date HR **formally accepts** the resignation in HRMS (not the date the employee drafts the email).

**Example:**
- Employee submits resignation: **Monday, 10 February 2025**
- Manager recommends accept: **11 February 2025**
- HR accepts in HRMS: **12 February 2025** ← **Notice start**
- L3 (60 days): LWD = **13 April 2025** (60 calendar days from 12 Feb)

### 3.2 Last Working Day (LWD)

- Calculated as start date + notice period in **calendar days** unless HR specifies working days for specific contracts  
- If LWD falls on weekend/holiday, previous working day is LWD unless employee requests next working day (manager approval)  
- HR sends official LWD confirmation email within **24 hours** of acceptance

## 4. Notice During Probation

| Scenario | Notice |
|----------|--------|
| Employee resigns in month 2 of probation | 30 days |
| Company releases during probation | 30 days (salary in lieu optional) |
| Confirmation at 6 months | Band notice applies for future separations |
| Probation extended | Still 30 days until confirmed |

**No PL during notice period** if employee resigns during probation after confirmation — see Leave Policy.

## 5. Garden Leave

Company may place employee on **garden leave** during notice (paid, no office attendance):

| Condition | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| Eligibility | L4+ with access to sensitive data, or conflict situations |
| Pay | Full salary continues |
| Benefits | Active until LWD |
| System access | Revoked or read-only |
| Duration | Remainder of notice or portion thereof |
| Employee obligation | Available for KT calls if requested (max 2 hrs/week) |

**Example:** L5 manager resigns to join direct competitor → 90-day notice, garden leave from day 31–90 with weekly 1-hour KT call.

## 6. Notice Waiver & Shortening

### 6.1 Employee Request to Shorten

- Submit written request with proposed LWD and reason  
- Requires: Manager + HRBP + Department Head approval  
- Company may accept with **notice buyout** (employee pays recovery) OR waive for critical personal reasons (medical, relocation abroad)  

### 6.2 Company-Initiated Shortening

- Company may release early with **salary in lieu of notice** for remainder days  
- Formula: `(Monthly Gross / 30) × Remaining Notice Days` paid as one-time payment  

**Example:** 60-day notice, released after 30 days  
Salary in lieu = (₹1,00,000 / 30) × 30 = **₹1,00,000** extra in FnF.

---

## 7. Notice Buyout (Employee-Initiated)

When employee cannot serve full notice:

| Option | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Recovery from FnF** | Company deducts shortfall from final settlement |
| **Cash buyout** | Employee pays before LWD; no deduction from FnF |
| **Mutual waiver** | Company waives partial notice (no payment either way) — rare |

**Buyout rate:** Daily gross = Monthly Gross / 30

**Example:** L4 employee, gross ₹1,50,000/month, serves 40 of 60 days  
Shortfall = 20 days  
Buyout = (1,50,000 / 30) × 20 = **₹1,00,000**

## 8. Notice Period & Leave

| Leave Type | During Notice (Employee Resignation) |
|------------|--------------------------------------|
| Privilege Leave (PL) | **Not permitted** |
| Casual Leave | Permitted (max 2 days total during notice) |
| Sick Leave | Permitted with medical certificate |
| Comp Off | Must be used before notice starts; not during notice |
| Public Holiday | Counts toward notice calendar |
| Optional/Floating Holiday | Counts toward notice calendar |

**Extension rule:** If employee takes **> 3 consecutive SL days** during notice, notice extends by same number of days (with valid certificate).

**Example:** 60-day notice starting 1 March, LWD 30 April. Employee on SL 10–14 March (5 days) → LWD shifts to **5 May**.

## 9. Notice Period & Work From Home

| Band | WFH During Notice |
|------|-------------------|
| L1 – L3 | Office required unless manager approves exception |
| L4 – L6 | Up to 50% WFH with manager approval |
| Garden leave | WFH not applicable — employee off active duty |

Unapproved WFH during notice may be treated as unauthorized absence.

---

## 10. Counter-Offer & Retention

If employee accepts counter-offer after submitting resignation:

- Resignation withdrawal must occur **before** LWD minus half of notice period  
- Maximum **one** withdrawal per rolling 12 months  
- If employee resigns again within **6 months** of withdrawal, notice period increases by **15 days** (penalty clause in acknowledgment)  

**Example:** L3 standard 60-day notice → second resignation within 6 months → **75-day notice**.

## 11. Termination for Cause — Notice

| Severity | Company Notice |
|----------|----------------|
| Gross misconduct (theft, violence, data breach) | **Immediate** — no notice, no salary in lieu |
| Serious misconduct (after inquiry) | 0–30 days per inquiry outcome |
| Performance (post-PIP) | 30–60 days per band |
| Redundancy | 60–90 days + severance per Exit Policy |

---

## 12. Notice in M&A / Restructuring

During acquisition integration:

- Existing notice terms honored for **90 days** post-close  
- After integration date, NovaTech band notice applies  
- Redundancy packages may substitute notice with salary in lieu + severance  

---

## 13. Legal & Contractual Overrides

- Employment agreement may specify longer notice for specialized roles (e.g., 120 days for Chief Architect)  
- **Higher of** agreement or policy applies  
- Statutory requirements under Shops & Establishments Act (Karnataka) observed where applicable  

---

## 14. Manager Responsibilities During Notice

1. Accept resignation professionally within 2 days  
2. Publish KT plan within 5 days of HR acceptance  
3. Do not revoke system access before HR/IT schedule  
4. Conduct fair handover; no punitive assignment  
5. Complete manager section of exit form by LWD  

**Violation:** Managers who harass or constructively dismiss during notice may face disciplinary action.
