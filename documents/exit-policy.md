# Employee Exit & Separation Policy

**Company:** NovaTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.  
**Document ID:** HR-POL-EXT-005  
**Version:** 3.5  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Applies To:** All employees (voluntary and involuntary separation)

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the process, timelines, and obligations when an employee separates from NovaTech Solutions — whether by resignation, termination, retirement, or mutual separation.

---

## 2. Types of Separation

| Type | Initiated By | Notice Required |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| Voluntary Resignation | Employee | Yes (per band) |
| Involuntary Termination | Company | Per labor law + policy |
| Mutual Separation | Both | As per agreement |
| Absconding | Employee (unauthorized absence) | N/A |
| Retirement | Employee (age) | 90 days recommended |
| End of Contract | Automatic | As per contract |

---

## 3. Notice Period

| Employee Band | Notice Period | Garden Leave Option |
|---------------|---------------|---------------------|
| L1 – L2 (Intern to Associate) | 30 days | Rare |
| L3 – L4 (Engineer to Team Lead) | 60 days | Manager discretion |
| L5 – L6 (Manager to Director) | 90 days | Common for sensitive roles |
| Probation (unconfirmed) | 30 days | No |

**Notice starts:** Date resignation is **accepted in writing** by HR (HRMS workflow).

**Example:** L3 engineer submits resignation 5 March → Accepted 6 March → LWD (Last Working Day) = **5 May** (60 calendar days; adjusted if holidays — HR confirms).

---

## 4. Resignation Process

### 4.1 Steps

1. Employee submits resignation on HRMS → Resignation module  
2. Manager acknowledgment within **2 working days**  
3. HR schedules exit discussion within **5 working days**  
4. KT (Knowledge Transfer) plan created — min **5 sessions** for IC roles  
5. Asset return checklist generated  
6. Exit interview scheduled (optional but encouraged)  
7. Full & Final settlement processed post LWD  

### 4.2 Withdrawal of Resignation

- Allowed **once** per 12-month period  
- Must request before LWD minus notice/2 (e.g., 60-day notice → withdraw by day 30)  
- Requires manager + HRBP approval  
- Not allowed if replacement already joined  

---

## 5. Knowledge Transfer (KT)

| Role Complexity | Minimum KT Duration | Deliverables |
|-----------------|---------------------|--------------|
| Individual Contributor | 2 weeks | Handover doc, repo access transfer |
| Tech Lead / Architect | 4 weeks | Architecture notes, runbooks |
| Manager | 4–8 weeks | Team transition plan, client intros |

**KT document template:** Available on HRMS → Exit Hub.

Failure to complete reasonable KT may delay experience letter (not salary/FNF if notice was served).

---

## 6. Exit Interview

- Conducted by HR (not direct manager)  
- Format: 30-minute confidential discussion or online survey  
- Topics: reasons for leaving, manager effectiveness, compensation, growth  
- Aggregated insights shared with leadership quarterly — no individual attribution

## 7. Asset Return & Access Revocation

### 7.1 Return Checklist

| Asset | Due Date | Penalty if Lost |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| Laptop + charger | LWD | ₹50,000 or asset value |
| Employee ID / access card | LWD | ₹500 |
| Monitor (if issued) | LWD | Full replacement cost |
| Company credit card | LWD + 2 days | Card blocked + recovery |
| Books / training materials | LWD | As per invoice |

### 7.2 IT Access

- **LWD 6:00 PM IST:** Email auto-forward to manager (30 days)  
- **LWD + 1 day:** All system access revoked  
- **Exceptions:** Pre-approved for KT support — max 7 days post-LWD with read-only access

## 8. Full & Final Settlement (FnF)

### 8.1 Components

**Credits:**
- Pro-rated salary for days worked in exit month  
- Unused Privilege Leave encashment (max 30 days at basic rate)  
- Pending approved reimbursements  
- Gratuity (if ≥ 5 years continuous service)  
- Pro-rated bonus (if employed ≥ 6 months in performance year and policy-eligible)  

**Debits:**
- Notice period shortfall (see Section 9)  
- Asset recovery  
- Outstanding salary advance / loan  
- Tax (TDS on encashment and bonus)  

### 8.2 Timeline

| Exit Type | FnF Timeline |
|-----------|--------------|
| Voluntary (complete notice) | Within **30 days** of LWD |
| Termination | Within **45 days** of LWD |
| Absconding | After legal notice period + investigation |

**Payment mode:** NEFT to registered bank account only.

### 8.3 FnF Example

**Employee:** Band L3, Basic ₹55,000/month, Gross ₹1,35,000/month  
**LWD:** 30 April  
**Days worked in April:** 30 (full month)  
**Unused PL:** 12 days  
**Notice served:** Full 60 days ✅  

| Item | Amount (INR) |
|------|--------------|
| April salary (gross) | 1,35,000 |
| PL encashment (55,000/30 × 12) | 22,000 |
| Pending reimbursement | 4,500 |
| **Gross credits** | **1,61,500** |
| Less: TDS (approx.) | (18,000) |
| Less: Employee PF | (6,600) |
| **Net FnF (approx.)** | **~1,36,900** |

*(Bonus paid separately in August if eligible.)*

## 9. Notice Period Shortfall

If employee leaves before completing notice:

**Recovery formula:**  
`Shortfall Days × (Monthly Gross / 30)`

| Shortfall | Typical Action |
|-----------|----------------|
| 1–15 days | Recovery from FnF |
| 16–30 days | Recovery + warning letter |
| > 30 days | Recovery + potential legal notice |

**Buyout:** Company may waive recovery if employee pays buyout amount OR mutual agreement signed.

**Example:** 60-day notice, employee leaves after 40 days → 20 days shortfall  
Recovery = (1,35,000 / 30) × 20 = **₹90,000** deducted from FnF.

## 10. Involuntary Termination

### 10.1 Grounds

- Misconduct ( theft, harassment, fraud)  
- Performance (after PIP failure — min 60-day PIP)  
- Redundancy / restructuring  
- BGV failure post-joining  

### 10.2 Process

- Show-cause notice (except summary dismissal for gross misconduct)  
- Internal committee review for POSH/misconduct cases  
- Employee may respond within **5 working days**  
- Separation letter issued with reason code  

**Severance (redundancy only):**  
`15 days' gross salary × years of service` (minimum 3 months' gross if tenure < 2 years).

**Example:** 4 years tenure, gross ₹1,20,000/month  
Severance = 15 × (1,20,000/30) × 4 = 15 × 4,000 × 4 = **₹2,40,000**

---

## 11. Absconding

**Definition:** Continuous unapproved absence for **≥ 3 working days** without communication.

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 1–3 | Manager + HR attempt contact (call, email, SMS) |
| Day 4 | Absconding letter via registered post + email |
| Day 7 | Employment terminated; access revoked |
| FnF | Held pending asset recovery; PL encashment forfeited per labor advisor guidance |

|----------|----------|-----------|

| Experience letter | With relieving letter | Standard template |

| Gratuity payment | Within 30 days of eligibility | 5+ years service |

---

## 13. Post-Exit Obligations

- **NDA & non-solicit:** Survives 12 months (non-solicit of employees/clients)  
- **IP assignment:** All work product remains company property  
- **Reference checks:** HR provides dates and designation only (standard policy)  
- **Rehire:** Eligible after **6 months** unless on do-not-hire list  

---

## 14. Retirement

- **Retirement age:** 60 years (extendable to 62 with board approval for critical roles)  
- **Notice:** 90 days recommended  
- **Gratuity:** Full eligibility if 5+ years  
- **Leave encashment:** Max 30 days PL  
- **Medical insurance:** Company group policy ceases on LWD; COBRA-like extension not offered in India entity
